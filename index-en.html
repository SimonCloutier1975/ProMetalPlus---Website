About Pro-Métal Plus

Since opening in 1991, Pro-Métal Plus has operated its plant in the Deschambault-Grondines industrial park (Portneuf County), Quebec. The company specializes in processing metals such as steel, stainless steel, aluminum and copper.

Our team of more than 110 dynamic and skilled employees designs, fabricates, welds, sand- or glass-blasts, applies the specified paint and installs a wide range of industrial parts and equipment, according to our clients' specifications.

We also provide qualified, specialized personnel on a rental basis. We support your planned production shutdowns in many sectors: aluminum smelters, paper mills, mines, cement plants and any other type of plant requiring the specializations and skills we are qualified for.

Sister company: BIOmasse Plus

Pro-Métal Plus was founded in 1991 and is located at 12 Boulevard des Sources in the Deschambault-Grondines industrial park.

      Law 25 · Protection of personal information

      Privacy policy

      The adoption of Law 25, the Act to modernize legislative provisions as regards the protection of personal information, requires all organizations to be transparent and accountable in how they handle personal information, and to ensure that it is collected, used, disclosed, retained, protected and destroyed in an appropriate manner that respects privacy. The new obligations came into force gradually between September 2022 and September 2024.

      The purpose of modernizing this law is to strengthen the protection of personal information held by businesses and increase trust in organizations, while taking into account constantly evolving technologies.

      This policy aims to be transparent about the protection methods Pro-Métal Plus uses for its employees, clients and suppliers, in order to better manage the handling of personal information.

      Personal information

      Any information that directly or indirectly identifies a natural person. Pro-Métal Plus collects, uses and discloses personal information about its team members in order to comply with laws and professional standards, administer performance management tools, administer, manage and enforce Pro-Métal Plus policies and its relationships with its employees, and establish and manage employment relationships.

      Security measures

      The Pro-Métal Plus team is committed to protecting the personal information of its employees, its team and its suppliers. To meet current personal information protection obligations, Pro-Métal Plus has implemented several measures.

      Person in charge of the protection of personal information

      This person must not only know all the personal information held by the organization, but also protect it adequately, report any potential personal data breach and notify the Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI).

      Information collected and how

      We receive, collect and store any information you enter on our website or provide to us in any other way.

      If you contact us, submit the form available on the site or inquire about our products or services, we collect your name, your company name (if applicable), your email address and your phone number.

      When you submit your résumé online at www.prometalplus.com, we also ask for your phone number in addition to the required information. Any additional personal information you include in your résumé serves to describe your skills, experience and job preferences.

      When you make a transaction on our website, we collect the personal information you give us, such as your name, address and email address. Your personal information will only be used for the specific purposes stated above.

      Why do we collect this information?

      • To provide and operate our services;
      • To provide our users with ongoing assistance and technical support;
      • To contact our visitors and users with general or personalized service-related notices and promotional messages;
      • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

      Information storage

      Our site is hosted on the Wix.com platform, which provides the online platform that allows us to offer you our products and services. Your data may be stored through Wix.com's data storage, databases and general applications, on secure servers behind a firewall.

      Retention period

      Information about the company's employees is kept for seven (7) years after employment ends. After that period, the data is deleted. The retention period for client information varies depending on the type of personal information. For details, refer to the complete inventory of personal information held.

      Destruction of information

      • Personal information on paper is completely shredded.
      • Digital personal information is completely deleted from devices (computers, phones, tablets, external hard drives), servers and cloud tools.
      • A destruction schedule is set according to the retention period of each category of information, and planned destruction dates are documented.
      • Destruction is carried out so that the information cannot be recovered or reconstructed.

      Managing confidentiality incidents

      Law 25, like Canadian federal law, requires businesses to keep a register of confidentiality incidents in order to document all events that affect or could affect the confidentiality and security of your data.

      Pro-Métal Plus has set up an incident register and a form to complete if you believe your personal information has been compromised. Following a confidentiality incident, Pro-Métal Plus is committed to implementing appropriate measures to prevent similar incidents from recurring. Incidents posing a risk of serious harm are reported to the CAI and to the individuals concerned.
